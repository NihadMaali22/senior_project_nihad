UNIVERSITY OF TECHNOLOGY — ACADEMIC REGULATIONS
Graduation Requirements
================================================================

Article 9: Degree Completion Requirements

Section 9.1: General Requirements
To be eligible for graduation with a Bachelor's degree, a student must satisfy ALL of the following requirements:
a) Complete a minimum of 132 credit hours (may vary by department, see Section 9.2).
b) Achieve a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.0 on a 4.0 scale.
c) Complete all required core courses for their major.
d) Complete all university general education requirements.
e) Complete the required internship(s) as specified by their department.
f) Complete the capstone/senior project (if required by the department).
g) Have no unresolved academic integrity violations.
h) Clear all financial obligations to the university.

Section 9.2: Department-Specific Credit Requirements
a) Computer Science (CS): 132 credit hours minimum, including 90 major credits.
b) Information Technology (IT): 130 credit hours minimum, including 85 major credits.
c) Electrical Engineering (EE): 140 credit hours minimum, including 95 major credits.
d) Business Administration (BA): 128 credit hours minimum, including 80 major credits.

Section 9.3: Time Limit
Students must complete their degree within 7 years of initial enrollment. Extensions may be granted by the Academic Affairs Committee under exceptional circumstances.

Article 10: Graduation Application

Section 10.1: Application Timeline
Students must apply for graduation by the following deadlines:
a) For June graduation: Apply by February 1st.
b) For January graduation: Apply by September 1st.
c) Late applications are subject to a late fee and may be deferred to the next graduation cycle.

Section 10.2: Graduation Audit
Upon receiving a graduation application, the Registrar's Office will conduct a graduation audit to verify that all requirements have been met. The student will be notified of the audit results within 30 days.

Section 10.3: Conditional Graduation
A student may be granted conditional graduation status if they are currently enrolled in their final required course(s) at the time of application, provided their projected grades would satisfy all requirements.

Article 11: Honors and Distinctions

Section 11.1: Latin Honors
a) Cum Laude: Cumulative GPA of 3.50 — 3.69
b) Magna Cum Laude: Cumulative GPA of 3.70 — 3.89
c) Summa Cum Laude: Cumulative GPA of 3.90 — 4.00

Section 11.2: Dean's List
Students who achieve a semester GPA of 3.50 or higher while completing at least 12 credit hours are placed on the Dean's List for that semester.

Section 11.3: Department Honors
Individual departments may award departmental honors to students who demonstrate exceptional performance in their major courses and complete an honors thesis or project.

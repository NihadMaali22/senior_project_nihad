UNIVERSITY OF TECHNOLOGY — ACADEMIC REGULATIONS
Course Withdrawal Policy
================================================================

Article 16: Course Withdrawal

Section 16.1: Withdrawal Period
a) The withdrawal period begins after the add/drop period ends (week 3) and extends to the end of week 10 of the semester.
b) After the withdrawal deadline, no withdrawals are permitted except in cases covered by Section 16.4.

Section 16.2: Withdrawal Procedure
To withdraw from a course, a student must:
a) Submit a withdrawal request through the online student portal.
b) Obtain approval from their academic advisor.
c) The withdrawal is effective on the date the request is approved.
d) A grade of "W" (Withdrawn) will be recorded on the transcript.

Section 16.3: Impact of Withdrawal

Section 16.3.1: Grade Impact
a) A "W" grade has NO impact on the student's GPA.
b) However, the credit hours are counted toward attempted hours.
c) Excessive withdrawals may affect financial aid eligibility.

Section 16.3.2: Maximum Withdrawals
a) A student may withdraw from a maximum of 6 courses during their entire academic program.
b) A student may not withdraw from the same course more than once.
c) If a student exceeds the maximum withdrawal limit, further withdrawals require department head approval.

Section 16.3.3: Course Load Impact
a) After withdrawal, if a student's remaining course load falls below 12 credit hours, they will be classified as part-time for that semester.
b) Part-time status may affect eligibility for certain university services and financial aid.

Section 16.4: Special Withdrawal (Medical/Emergency)

Section 16.4.1: Medical Withdrawal
a) Students may apply for a medical withdrawal at any time during the semester by submitting a medical certificate from a licensed physician to the Registrar's Office.
b) Medical withdrawals are recorded as "WM" on the transcript and have no impact on GPA or withdrawal count.

Section 16.4.2: Emergency Withdrawal
a) In cases of documented emergencies (family emergency, natural disaster, etc.), a student may apply for an emergency withdrawal through the Dean of Students office.
b) Emergency withdrawals are recorded as "WE" and do not count toward the withdrawal limit.

Article 17: Incomplete Grades

Section 17.1: Issuance
An Incomplete grade ("I") may be assigned when a student has completed at least 75% of the coursework but is unable to complete the remainder due to documented circumstances beyond their control.

Section 17.2: Resolution
a) The student must complete the remaining work within 60 days of the last day of the semester.
b) If the work is not completed within the deadline, the Incomplete grade automatically converts to an "F".
c) The instructor may grant a one-time extension of up to 30 additional days.

Section 17.3: Restrictions
a) A student may not re-register for a course in which they have an active Incomplete grade.
b) A maximum of two Incomplete grades may be active simultaneously.

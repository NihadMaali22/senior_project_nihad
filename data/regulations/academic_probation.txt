UNIVERSITY OF TECHNOLOGY — ACADEMIC REGULATIONS
Academic Probation and Academic Standing Policy
================================================================

Article 1: Academic Standing Classification

Section 1.1: Good Standing
A student is considered in good academic standing when their cumulative Grade Point Average (GPA) is 2.0 or higher on a 4.0 scale. Students in good standing have full access to all university services and registration privileges.

Section 1.2: Academic Warning
A student receives an academic warning when their cumulative GPA falls between 1.75 and 1.99 for the first time. The student will be notified in writing and advised to seek academic counseling.

Section 1.3: Academic Probation
A student is placed on academic probation when:
a) Their cumulative GPA falls below 2.0 for a second consecutive semester.
b) Their semester GPA falls below 1.5 in any single semester.
c) They fail more than 50% of their registered credit hours in a single semester.

Article 2: Probation Consequences

Section 2.1: Registration Restrictions
Students on academic probation are subject to the following restrictions:
a) Maximum course load is reduced to 12 credit hours per semester (from normal 18).
b) Students may not register for internship courses while on probation.
c) Students may not register for capstone/senior project courses while on probation.
d) Students must obtain advisor approval before registration each semester.

Section 2.2: Required Actions
Students on probation must:
a) Meet with their academic advisor within the first two weeks of the semester.
b) Develop an academic improvement plan.
c) Attend all required tutoring sessions if recommended.
d) Raise their cumulative GPA to 2.0 or above by the end of the probation semester.

Article 3: Academic Dismissal

Section 3.1: Grounds for Dismissal
A student may be dismissed from the university when:
a) They fail to raise their GPA above 2.0 after two consecutive probation semesters.
b) Their cumulative GPA falls below 1.0 at any point.
c) They receive academic warnings in three or more semesters.

Section 3.2: Dismissal Appeal
Students who are dismissed may submit a written appeal to the Academic Appeals Committee within 15 business days of receiving the dismissal notice. The appeal must include:
a) A written statement explaining the circumstances.
b) A proposed academic recovery plan.
c) Supporting documentation (medical records, etc.) if applicable.

Section 3.3: Reinstatement
Dismissed students may apply for reinstatement after one full academic year (two semesters). Reinstatement is not guaranteed and is subject to approval by the Academic Affairs Committee.

Article 4: GPA Improvement

Section 4.1: Course Retake Policy
Students may retake a course in which they earned a grade of D or lower. The higher grade will be used in GPA calculation, but both attempts appear on the transcript.

Section 4.2: Maximum Retakes
A student may retake the same course a maximum of two times (three total attempts). After three attempts, special permission from the department head is required.

Section 4.3: Grade Replacement
When a course is retaken, the new grade replaces the old grade in the GPA calculation, provided the new grade is higher. If the retake grade is lower, the original grade remains in the GPA calculation.

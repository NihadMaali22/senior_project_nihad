UNIVERSITY OF TECHNOLOGY — ACADEMIC REGULATIONS
Course Registration Policy
================================================================

Article 5: Registration Procedures

Section 5.1: Registration Period
Course registration is conducted during the designated registration period before the start of each semester. The registration period is announced at least two weeks in advance through the university portal.

Section 5.2: Registration Eligibility
To register for courses, a student must:
a) Have an active enrollment status.
b) Have no outstanding financial obligations to the university.
c) Not be under academic dismissal.
d) Have met with their academic advisor (if required by their standing).

Section 5.3: Course Load Limits
a) The standard course load is 15-18 credit hours per semester.
b) Students with a GPA of 3.5 or higher may request an overload of up to 21 credit hours.
c) Students on academic probation are limited to 12 credit hours per semester.
d) Summer semester course load is limited to 9 credit hours.

Article 6: Prerequisites and Course Requirements

Section 6.1: Prerequisite Enforcement
a) Prerequisites are strictly enforced. A student may not register for a course unless all prerequisites have been completed with the minimum required grade.
b) The default minimum grade for prerequisites is D unless otherwise specified in the course catalog.
c) Prerequisite checks are performed automatically during registration.

Section 6.2: Minimum GPA Requirements
Certain courses require a minimum cumulative GPA for registration:
a) Internship courses require a minimum GPA of 2.0.
b) Capstone/Senior Project courses require a minimum GPA of 2.0.
c) Advanced elective courses may have individual GPA requirements as specified in the course description.

Section 6.3: Minimum Credit Hour Requirements
Some courses require a minimum number of completed credit hours:
a) Internship 1 requires a minimum of 60 completed credit hours.
b) Internship 2 requires a minimum of 90 completed credit hours AND completion of Internship 1.
c) Capstone Project requires a minimum of 110 completed credit hours.
d) Senior-level courses (400-level) require at least 75 completed credit hours.

Article 7: Add/Drop Period

Section 7.1: Add Period
Students may add courses during the first week of the semester without penalty. Adding courses after the first week requires department head approval.

Section 7.2: Drop Period
a) Students may drop courses without academic penalty during the first two weeks of the semester (add/drop period). Dropped courses will not appear on the transcript.
b) After the add/drop period and before the withdrawal deadline, students must follow the official withdrawal procedure.

Section 7.3: Course Swap
Students may swap courses (simultaneous add and drop) during the add/drop period, subject to seat availability and prerequisite requirements.

Article 8: Course Conflicts and Scheduling

Section 8.1: Time Conflicts
Students may not register for courses with overlapping schedules. The registration system will automatically prevent time conflicts.

Section 8.2: Maximum Sections
A student may register for only one section of a given course per semester.

Arab American University – Palestine
Student Violations Disciplinary Regulation – 2024
Approved by the Board of Directors on 28/02/2024
CHAPTER ONE
DEFINITIONS
Article (1): This regulation shall be named the "Student Violations Disciplinary Regulation – 2024" and shall enter into force from the date of its approval by the Board of Directors.
Article (2): The following words and expressions, wherever they appear in this Regulation, shall have the meanings assigned to them below, unless the context indicates otherwise:
The University:  Arab American University, including its premises in Tulkarm/Jenin, all areas within the university fence in addition to the university campus, the dental clinics of the Dental Medicine Center in the Ramallah/God's Blessing area, any location where teaching or regulated university activities take place, any center or building or external office affiliated with the university, and the university's liaison offices.
The Student:  The person regularly enrolled in an academic program at Arab American University, or a person admitted or expected to complete their enrollment for one academic semester or more, in accordance with the university's regulations and instructions, which include all scientific degrees at the university.
The University Family:  The administration of the university, its owners and employees, and the enrolled students.
The President:  The President of the University.
The Dean:  The Dean of Student Affairs, or the Dean of any college at the university as indicated by the context of this Regulation.
The Head of Department:  Any academic department head at the university.
Council of Deans:  The Council of Deans of Arab American University.
Disciplinary Committee:  The Student Violations Disciplinary Committee (Primary).
Appeals Committee:  The Student Cases Appeals Committee.
Board of Directors:  The Board of Directors of Arab American University Company.
Chairman of the Board:  The Chairman of the Board of Directors of Arab American University Company.
CHAPTER TWO
ACADEMIC VIOLATIONS
Article (3): Any act that negatively affects the educational process shall be considered an academic violation entailing a disciplinary penalty in accordance with this Regulation. This includes the following:
1.	Disrupting the order and discipline required by lectures, discussions, seminars, the educational process, and any other academic or educational activities related to the university, whether inside or outside the university campus.
2.	Disrupting the conduct of examinations and their regulations.
3.	Cheating in examinations, attempting or inciting it, including the student using or attempting to use unauthorized materials or information during examinations, or attempting to offer them to others during examinations, including hiding or retaining written or recorded information for use inside the examination room, or violating the instructions of the supervisor or examinations monitor, entering unauthorized papers, notes, devices, or materials of any kind — written, visual, electronic, or audio — into the examination room to disrupt examinations without prior permission from the responsible supervisor, changing the assigned seat in the examination hall without permission from the examinations monitor, obtaining assistance from students in the examination hall after the beginning of the examination, modifying grades and marks on papers or examination scripts before their submission to the administration, receiving information from students or from other students inside the examination hall in any form, tampering electronically with examinations held by the university in any form, failing to comply with the supervisor's orders and instructions and behaving inappropriately during the examination, standing in the corridors of the examination hall and attempting to disturb or disrupt the examination, exchanging answer papers during the examination, assisting another student in committing any of the above-mentioned violations.
4.	Submitting or attempting to submit examinations from another person and/or usurping the identity of the student for the purpose of taking the examination in their place.
5.	Inciting or participating in inciting the boycott of lectures, laboratories, discussions, seminars, or examinations.
6.	Entering teaching halls, laboratories, discussions, or scientific seminars for the purpose of inciting students to leave the halls.
7.	Appropriating another's work as one's own, including literary theft of research, data, or information, and using references and sources in examinations, dissertations, or any academic requirement for a course, appropriating another's work without attribution and claiming ownership of the appropriated information that exceeds 25% of the appropriated information according to a report issued by the head of department, using another's work including ideas, designs, data, research, or parts thereof, or information or references or results of research without citing the complete reference and claiming intellectual ownership thereof, and submitting research built on another's work in full or in part in violation of scientific integrity, copied by the student from another — whether by purchase, borrowing, or obtaining them with the consent of the author attributing them to themselves, submitting work for courses previously completed in a prior semester without prior notice of that, cooperating with another to accomplish academic work without prior permission from a member of the teaching staff, whether in the primary or final stage of academic work, assisting another student in committing any of the aforementioned violations.
8.	Committing any academic violation using artificial intelligence means, including by way of example and not limitation: submitting written reports or analyses using artificial intelligence tools, using artificial intelligence in submitting examinations whether in person or remotely, using artificial intelligence-generated data and claiming its authorship, and assisting other students in committing any of the violations mentioned in this item.
9.	Violating the academic regulations and instructions of the university's departments and various colleges.
10.	Committing any other act that would disrupt the university's systems, regulations, and academic decisions, or any act deemed by the Disciplinary Committee to be an academic violation.
CHAPTER THREE
BEHAVIORAL VIOLATIONS
Article (4): The following acts and behaviors are considered behavioral violations entailing a disciplinary penalty for their perpetrators in accordance with this Regulation:
11.	Violating the university's non-academic regulations, instructions, and decisions.
12.	Defaming the reputation of the university verbally or in action, inside or outside the university.
13.	Violating the sanctity of the blessed month of Ramadan, including the public breaking of the fast.
14.	Insulting religious identity and/or using words or performing actions or making gestures that lead to offending the feelings and beliefs — religious, national — of others.
15.	Committing a crime against public morals, honor, and general decorum inside or outside the university, or instigating its commission.
16.	Participating, inciting, coordinating, or intervening (assisting) or participating in a group activity inside the university without prior permission from the competent authorities at the university.
17.	Using university buildings, facilities, or any affiliated buildings for purposes other than those intended, or using them without prior permission from the competent authorities at the university.
18.	Collecting donations of any kind (cash or in-kind) without obtaining prior permission from the competent authorities at the university, or misusing the donations that the university has allowed to be collected with its permission.
19.	Damaging, attempting to damage, or misusing any of the university's movable or fixed property, or the property of others inside the university.
20.	Forgery, participation, intervention, or incitement in forging university documents, or using forged documents, which includes tampering with or altering any university document, using a forged document of any kind — whether it originated from the university or was submitted to the university — assisting another student in committing any of the aforementioned violations.
21.	Theft of university-owned funds or property or for another, or participation in their theft or incitement or coordination or intervention in the theft, whether inside or outside the university in connection with any activity in which the university participates or any activity it organizes.
22.	Obstructing the university's educational process, including blocking students and attempting to prevent them from attending lectures or examinations, using threats and using force or any other means, preventing students or attempting to prevent students from entering the university or participating in university activities whether academic or otherwise, including celebrations, sporting activities, and other university activities.
23.	Unlawfully entering offices, rooms, buildings, halls, or university facilities.
24.	Failing to comply with the legitimate orders of a member of the teaching staff or the administrative official to leave the classroom or the relevant employee's office.
25.	Obstructing the work of investigative committees or any other academic committees in any form, including resorting to intimidation, threats, or harassment.
26.	Carrying weapons inside the university whether the weapon is real (genuine or replica) or any material that threatens the safety of others or the threat of using them.
27.	Smoking inside university buildings.
28.	Consuming alcohol or narcotic substances of any type inside the university or any of its affiliated facilities.
29.	Promoting narcotic substances of any type or trading in them in any form inside the university or for students outside the university.
30.	Performing any act with the potential to harass any member of the university family or its visitors, and subjecting them to intimidation, or insulting, exposing them to physical danger or psychological harm.
31.	Distributing flyers or posting them or announcing them on the walls of colleges or gathering signatures without obtaining prior approval from the competent authorities at the university.
32.	Assaulting or attempting to assault any member of the university family by beating, injuring, intimidating, threatening, insulting, or instigating it.
33.	Using the university's name and claiming to represent it without written authorization from the competent authorities.
34.	Defaming the reputation of the university or the university family through newspapers, daily publications, bulletins, or any form of printed material, social media, or any other media platform.
35.	Publishing harmful statements about the university via social media or any other channel.
36.	Editing, modifying, or distributing audio or video recordings or photos of other students or university employees or any member of the university family without prior permission from them.
37.	Distributing or exchanging any materials containing nudity and general moral offenses including pornographic films and indecent images inside the university.
38.	Participating in disputes or acts of violence inside the university or instigating or intervening in them.
39.	Providing false testimony and/or false oath and/or denying the truth, whether in whole or in part, before an investigative committee or any body having the authority to receive testimony on behalf of the university.
40.	Disparagement and/or ridicule and/or directed criticism of the university's bodies and/or councils and/or any member of the university family and the outsiders inside the university.
41.	Failure to report any incident they have knowledge of to the responsible authority.
42.	Violating the university's applicable dress code.
43.	Obstructing or attempting to obstruct the university's administrative work.
44.	Forging or attempting to forge student council elections, and attempting to influence students through illegal means for electoral purposes, including threats, purchase, or attempting to purchase votes in any form — electronic or otherwise.
45.	Violating previously issued disciplinary decisions.
46.	Failure to comply with the competent authorities at the university, including campus security, in presenting the university ID card or personal identity card.
47.	Committing any act (inside or outside the university) that constitutes a crime under the applicable laws in Palestine, and is considered by the administration's judicial decision as sufficient for the application of the penalties specified thereon in Chapter Four without the need to refer to an investigative committee.
48.	Committing any violation of any of the university's applicable systems.
49.	Committing any other act that would disrupt the order and security at the university and/or any act deemed by the Disciplinary Committee to be a behavioral violation.
CHAPTER FOUR
PENALTIES
Article (5)(a): If the student's commission of any of the violations specified in Articles (3) and (4) of this Regulation is proven, one of the following penalties shall be imposed:
50.	Written warning.
51.	Expulsion from the classroom or examination hall and summoning campus security to enforce this if necessary.
52.	Deprivation for a specified period from using a service provided by one or more of the university facilities in which the violation was committed.
53.	Deprivation for a specified period from practicing a sport or one or more of the student activities in which the violation was committed.
54.	Warning in four degrees: first, second, double, and final.
55.	A fine no less than the value of the thing or items damaged by the student.
56.	Failure in the course or in one of its assessments — First Exam, Second Exam, Quizzes, or Final.
57.	Failure in all course subjects of the semester.
58.	Temporary suspension from the university for one academic semester or more.
59.	Final dismissal from the university.
60.	Delaying the decision to grant the student the academic degree for a period not exceeding one academic year.
61.	Cancellation of the degree decision if it is found that there was fraud or forgery in the requirements for obtaining it.
62.	Penalties for students in the Colleges of Dentistry and Human Medicine: (a) Dean's authority in core courses: For core courses that count within the GPA and whose passing is a condition for moving from one year to another or for completing graduation requirements, the Dean has the authority to impose the maximum penalty represented by recording a zero mark for the student in the assessment in which the violation occurred. (b) Final Exam and Makeup Exam: If the Dean decides to impose a penalty from the final examination due to cheating, and the instructions specific to the degree permit holding a makeup exam for graduation purposes, the grade adopted for the student in the makeup exam is calculated at a maximum of (70%) from the final exam grade, and (70%) of the final exam grade is deducted for the makeup exam, regardless of the actual result. (c) Referring serious violations to the student violations disciplinary committee: If the Dean estimates that the violation requires a penalty more severe than those falling within their authority, the student is referred to the student violations disciplinary committee to look into the matter and take the appropriate decision according to the instructions and in accordance with what Article (8)–(b) permits.
Article (5)(b): In addition to the penalties specified in the previous paragraph, a decision may be taken obligating the student who committed the violation to compensate the university or others for the damages caused to the university or others as a result of the violation committed.
Article (6): (a) It is not permissible to combine two or more disciplinary penalties from those specified in this Regulation for the same violation. (b) In the event of imposing the penalty of temporary suspension from the university, the student's ID is withdrawn and they are prevented from entering the university campus during the suspension period, except with prior permission from the Dean of Student Affairs. (c) In cases of repetition, the penalty is aggravated. (d) Penalties for academic violations are imposed cumulatively in a manner that may lead to the penalty of dismissal from the university. (e) The violations specified in paragraphs (5, 10, 11, 15, 16, 18, 19, 20, 22, 24, 25, 27, 28, 30) of Article (4) carry the penalty of dismissal from the university.
Article (7): If the student subject to the effective final warning penalty commits any of the violations specified in Articles (3) or (4) of this Regulation, one of the penalties specified in paragraphs 9 and 10 of Section (a) of Article (5) of this Regulation shall be imposed on them.
Article (8): (a) If the student is caught during the examination in one of the courses attempting cheating or attempting to cheat, the college dean shall impose on them, based on the instructor's recommendation, a penalty from the penalties specified in paragraphs 5 or 7 of Section (a) of Article 5 of this Regulation. (b) If the Dean finds the violation requires a more severe penalty than those within their authority, the matter is referred to the student violations disciplinary committee following the established procedures. (c) In case of the student repeating cheating or attempting to cheat, one of the penalties specified in paragraphs 8, 9, or 10 of Section (a) of Article 5 of this Regulation is imposed by the Disciplinary Committee. (d) One of the penalties in paragraphs 9 or 10 of Section (a) of Article 5 of this Regulation is imposed on every student who entered the exam to sit it in place of another, and also on the student who entered in place of another student who should have been sitting for the exam, by the Disciplinary Committee. (e) If the person who entered the examination hall was from outside the university, they are referred to the competent judicial authorities.
Article (9): The suspended student shall not earn academic credit for any subjects they study during the suspension period at any other university.
Article (10): (a) The student against whom a penalty of suspension from the university (temporary or final) has been issued shall not be reimbursed the paid tuition fees. (b) The student against whom a final suspension from the university has been issued has the right to obtain a transcript of the subjects they studied, provided that the transcript shows the suspension penalty, after paying all dues owed to the university.
Article (11): The authority to impose disciplinary penalties on students is specified as follows: (a) For a member of the teaching staff: the right to impose the penalties specified in paragraphs (1) and (2) of Section (a) of Article (5) of this Regulation. (b) For the Dean: the right to impose the penalties specified in paragraphs (1) to (7) of Section (a) of Article (5) of this Regulation in cases where the student commits any of the academic violations specified in Article (3) of this Regulation. (c) The penalties specified in paragraphs 8–12 of Section (a) of Article (5) are not imposed except through a recommendation by the Disciplinary Committee according to the procedures of this Regulation. (d) Notwithstanding what is provided in the previous paragraph and in exceptional urgent cases that do not permit delay, and within the highest authority in the university, the President of the University and not the appeals committee has the authority to impose the final dismissal penalty specified in paragraph (10) of Section (a) of Article (5) of this Regulation, if the student commits one of the behavioral violations specified in paragraphs (5, 16, 18, 19) of Article (4) of this Regulation. (e) In exceptional urgent cases related to a student committing one of the serious violations specified in this Regulation, the President of the University may issue a provisional decision preventing the violating student from entering university premises pending its referral to the competent Disciplinary Committee and the issuance of its recommendations regarding the violation.
Article (12): (a) The student referred to the Disciplinary Committee may not withdraw from their studies before the review of the complaint filed against them concludes. (b) Student graduation procedures are suspended pending the resolution of the violation they committed.
CHAPTER FIVE
INVESTIGATIVE COMMITTEES AND PROCEDURES
Article (13): The Council of Deans shall form, at the beginning of each academic year, two student violations disciplinary committees at the university — one primary and one secondary (appellate). This shall be done for each of the university's campuses in Jenin and Ramallah/God's Blessing.
Article (14): Each committee from the committees mentioned in the preceding article shall be composed of three members from the members of the teaching staff, one of whom is named as its rapporteur, provided that the committee's rapporteur is from the members of the teaching staff in the Faculty of Law at the university.
Article (15): (a) Students who commit violations are referred to the primary Disciplinary Committee by the President. (b) The referral of the student to the Disciplinary Committee is done before the penalty is discovered for six months or more. (c) The Disciplinary Committee has the right to summon all persons whose testimony may be useful in revealing the truth, whether they appeared spontaneously in the case referred to it or not, and questioning is the right of all members of the committee and the accused, and these testimonies are recorded in the session minutes. (d) If it is established by the Disciplinary Committee that others were involved in the violation, the committee's rapporteur must raise the matter to the President to refer the case to the primary Disciplinary Committee.
Article (16): (a) It is not permissible to refer any violation to the Disciplinary Committee if the Dean imposed a disciplinary penalty on the violating student from the penalties falling within their authority according to Section (b) of Article (11) of this Regulation for the same violation. (b) In the case the matter has been referred to the Disciplinary Committee, the Dean may not impose any disciplinary penalty from the penalties falling within their authority according to Section (b) of Article (11) of this Regulation for the same violation.
Article (17): (a) A copy of the referral decision is sent to the Dean of Student Affairs. (b) The Dean of Student Affairs provides the primary committee's rapporteur with a report on the student's behavior referred to it, so that the report includes the previous violations and the penalties previously imposed on them.
Article (18): The Disciplinary Committee's rapporteur invites its members to meet and is responsible for managing the committee's sessions, recording its minutes, and preparing reports.
Article (19): (a) The Disciplinary Committee shall hold its sessions to consider the case referred to it within a period not exceeding seven working days from the date the case was referred to it, and shall resolve it within a period not exceeding twenty days from the date the consideration began, with the university president having the right to grant it an equal additional period if deemed necessary. (b) In emergency cases and upon a decision from the President, the committee may convene a disciplinary session within (3) working days from the date the case was referred to it and resolve it within a period not exceeding (7) working days.
Article (20): (a) The Disciplinary Committee's rapporteur invites the accused students and witnesses via the Dean of Student Affairs by any appropriate means, including electronic notification or any means that achieves the purpose. (b) If the accused student is absent from the hearing twice consecutively despite being notified according to the system without a legitimate excuse accepted by the Disciplinary Committee, the committee considers the case as if the accused student were present. (c) The Disciplinary Committee must, when first meeting to examine a violation, establish the student's identity including their name and the college they belong to, and read out to them the violations charged against them, and the student's statements are recorded in the session minutes. (d) If the Disciplinary Committee finds during the examination of a violation that there are others accused in the violation, the committee's rapporteur must raise their names to the President to refer them to the primary Disciplinary Committee following established procedures. (e) Evidence in cases referred to the Disciplinary Committee is evaluated by all means of proof.
Article (21): The student accused of committing a violation has the right to submit any evidence before the committee, including calling witnesses to establish the soundness of their position.
Article (22): The primary Disciplinary Committee issues its recommendation by majority, which shall include a summary of the facts in the case and the reasons for conviction or acquittal and the applicable article of this Regulation, and in the case of conviction, the determination of the penalty and the proposed compensations if any.
Article (23): (a) The Disciplinary Committee raises its recommendations to the President, and these recommendations shall not become effective except upon their approval by the President, and the President's decision is final if the penalty imposed is not subject to appeal, or if no appeal is filed within the period specified in this Regulation. The President shall notify their decision to the competent authorities at the university and the concerned student is notified of the decision via the Dean of Student Affairs. (b) The decision issued by the President based on the recommendation of the Disciplinary Committee and subject to appeal shall not be executed during the period allowed for filing the appeal or during the appeal hearing period, except after final decisions are made therein.
Article (24): (a) Decisions to impose disciplinary penalties are kept in the student's file in the Dean of Student Affairs office, and the decision is communicated to the concerned college's dean and to the Admissions and Registration Office. (b) The dean of the concerned college must notify all decisions to impose penalties on any of their college's students to the Dean of Student Affairs and the Admissions and Registration Office; the Dean of Student Affairs handles notifying the student on whom those decisions were issued.
CHAPTER SIX
APPEALS AND OBJECTIONS TO DECISIONS
Article (25): The Council of Deans shall form, at the beginning of each academic year, two appeals committees to appeal the decisions approved by the President based on the recommendations of the primary Disciplinary Committee, one for each of the university's campuses in Jenin and Ramallah/God's Blessing. The appeals committee is composed of three members from the members of the teaching staff, one of whom is named as its rapporteur, with the condition that the committee's rapporteur is from the members of the teaching staff in the Faculty of Law at the university.
Article (26): (a) The appeal is submitted to the President via the Dean of Student Affairs in writing within fifteen days from the date the student is notified of the decision if the penalty imposed is subject to appeal. The university president refers the appeal to the appeals committee's rapporteur within two working days of receiving it.
Article (27): (a) The Appeals Committee shall issue its decision as soon as possible, within no more than twenty days from the date the committee's rapporteur receives the appeal, and ten working days from the date the committee's rapporteur receives the appeal in the emergency cases that do not permit delay. (b) The Appeals Committee may hear the witnesses who were heard before the Disciplinary Committee that recommended the contested penalty, and shall complete all other investigative procedures. (c) The Appeals Committee rules by: 1. Upholding the appealed decision issued by the President based on the Disciplinary Committee's recommendation if the appeal is found formally and/or substantively unacceptable. 2. Modifying or canceling the appealed decision issued by the President based on the Disciplinary Committee's recommendation while stating the reasons. (d) All decisions of the Appeals Committee are issued by majority. (e) Decisions issued by the Appellate Disciplinary Committee are final and are raised to the President for execution; those who refuse to execute them are subject to disciplinary accountability. (f) No appeal shall be prejudiced by appealing it.
Article (28): The disciplinary penalties specified in this Regulation are final except for those subject to appeal — except for the penalties specified in paragraphs (8), (9), (10), (11), and (12) of Section (a) of Article (5) of this Regulation.
CHAPTER SEVEN
GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article (29): The imposition of any of the penalties specified in this Regulation does not exempt the student from paying compensation for the damages they caused; the university has the right to recover these compensations.
Article (30): The regulations, instructions, and decisions applied at the university and issued by it are not considered an excuse for those who commit any violation, and this does not exempt the student from bearing responsibility.
Article (31): The Dean of Student Affairs may, after consulting and coordinating with the college dean to whom the student who executed the penalty belongs, suspend the disciplinary penalty imposed under the fifth paragraph of Section (a) of Article Five of this Regulation and mitigate it, based on what the holder of this privilege submits to the Dean of Student Affairs, requesting mitigation of the penalty imposed on them, if it is proven that they demonstrate good conduct and behavior. The decision is forwarded to the Admissions and Registration Office.
Article (32): (a) The Dean of Student Affairs and/or the relevant college dean has the right to impose a first warning penalty on the student in the event of smoking in university facilities. (b) The Dean of Student Affairs may, after consulting and coordinating with the college dean to whom the student belongs who received a warning due to smoking, remove this warning from the student after three months from its issuance date, if the student demonstrates good conduct and behavior.
Article (33): The President of the University may issue the executive and procedural decisions they deem necessary to implement the provisions of this Regulation in accordance with the decisions of the Board of Directors.
Article (34): This Regulation supersedes any previous regulation or instruction of the same subject.
Article (35): This Regulation is issued pursuant to a decision of the Board of Directors dated 28/02/2024 and enters into force from the date of its issuance and signing by the Chairman of the Board of Directors. The Board of Directors may introduce any amendments it deems necessary to this Regulation.
